# The Daily Operating Picture

The Daily Operating Picture (DOP) is Mongoose's flagship: the cross-domain shape of your
day in one place. It is not a dashboard of separate widgets. It is a synthesis that joins
calendar, weather, email, WhatsApp, tasks, files, home state, memory, system health and
receipts, and surfaces the connections BETWEEN them that no single-purpose tool can see.

See it: the cockpit Overview opens with it, or run `mongoose daily`, or GET /cockpit/daily.

## What it answers

- **Today's shape**: the spine of the day, timed events ahead, weather, the date.
- **Connections worth noticing**: the heart of it. An email or unanswered WhatsApp from
  someone you have a calendar event with today; weather that threatens an outdoor event;
  a file you just edited that relates to today's work. These cross-domain links are the
  reason Mongoose exists.
- **What needs you**: a single triage queue, ranked, joining approvals waiting, replies
  owed, actionable email, and repairs (degraded capabilities). Urgent ("now") before
  "soon".
- **What can wait**: real but non-urgent context.
- **What's broken**: capabilities not configured or failing, honestly.
- **What Mongoose did**: recent receipts.
- **Next smallest step**: the single most useful thing to do now.

## Facts before fluency

Every section is COMPUTED from real signals, not generated by a model. The connections,
the triage, what's broken, what changed, all come from your actual data. A model may
phrase the headline later, but it never invents what's waiting or what changed. Viewing
the DOP does not consume your "what changed" checkpoint (it peeks, it doesn't reset).

## Privacy

High-privacy email subjects are never surfaced in connections (an email from someone in
your calendar can be noted without exposing a sensitive subject line). The DOP reuses the
brief's gatherer, which already respects per-item privacy classes.

## Honest limits

The DOP is only as rich as the data flowing in. With no calendar/email/WhatsApp synced
yet, it shows today's shape and system health and says, honestly, that there's little to
connect. Its value grows as you connect domains (see docs/MAIL_SYNC.md, docs/DEVICES.md).
The cross-domain matching is currently term/contact based (good, not speculative); deeper
contact resolution is future work.
